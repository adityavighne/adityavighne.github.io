About Me

As a Senior Marketing Data Analyst, my expertise lies in bridging the gap between raw data and strategic business decisions. With over 8 years in the field, I've honed my skills in analyzing complex marketing datasets to uncover key trends, identify growth opportunities, and optimize campaign performance.

I am proficient in a wide array of analytical tools including **Power BI, DAX, Marketo, Google Analytics, Tableau, Qlik, and Adobe Analytics**. My work involves deep dives into customer segmentation, A/B testing, and comprehensive tracking of leads, campaigns, social media, and advertising data to understand and predict user behavior.

A highlight of my career includes the successful implementation of a new travel policy that significantly boosted sales, generating over $5M in a single quarter. I am passionate about leveraging Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to build predictive models that enhance marketing effectiveness and drive measurable results. I thrive on turning data into compelling narratives that inform and inspire action.
