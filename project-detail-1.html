Marketing Campaign ROI Optimization

This project focused on developing a robust analytics framework to comprehensively track, analyze, and optimize the Return on Investment (ROI) for multi-channel marketing campaigns. The primary objective was to move beyond simple vanity metrics and provide actionable insights that directly contributed to business growth.

I designed and implemented a series of interactive dashboards using **Power BI** and **Tableau**, integrating data from various sources including **Marketo, Google Analytics, CRM (Salesforce)**, and advertising platforms. These dashboards provided real-time visibility into campaign performance, allowing stakeholders to quickly identify trends, bottlenecks, and opportunities.

A key component of this project involved setting up and analyzing **A/B tests** across different campaign elements (e.g., ad creatives, landing page layouts, email subject lines). By meticulously tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) and applying statistical significance tests, we were able to identify winning variations that significantly improved conversion rates and reduced customer acquisition costs.

Furthermore, I leveraged **Machine Learning** techniques to build predictive models that identified high-potential customer segments for targeted campaigns. This proactive approach allowed the marketing team to allocate resources more effectively, leading to a substantial increase in qualified leads and overall campaign efficiency. The project also included a detailed report on the attribution models used to accurately credit conversions to the correct marketing touchpoints.

The successful implementation of this framework resulted in a **15% increase in overall marketing campaign ROI** within the first six months and provided the marketing team with a clear, data-driven methodology for continuous optimization.
